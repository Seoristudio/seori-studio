Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Seori Studio (Lee Seori) operates this website. This notice explains the optional measurement used on this site and how you can control it.

Information and purposes

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 may receive page views, pages and referrers, browser and device details, online identifiers such as its measurement cookies, and interactions such as scrolling, outbound-link clicks, and file downloads where enhanced measurement detects them. Google uses an IP address at collection to estimate location such as country or city, then says it discards the IP address before the data is logged. We use these reports to understand traffic and improve the site.

If you allow advertising measurement, Google Ads conversion measurement runs only on the Shop page and records clicks on designated shop links. We use that information to understand which shop links visitors choose. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, or other form details through these tags.

Cookies and your choices

Before you allow an optional category, this site does not load its Google Analytics or Google Ads tags. A first-party cookie named seori_site_consent stores only your analytics and advertising choices for six months so the banner does not have to ask again on each visit. You can change or withdraw those choices at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer. If you decline optional measurement, the site continues to work; analytics and Shop conversion measurement remain off. When you withdraw permission, the site blocks future measurement and attempts to remove Google measurement cookies already present on this site. Information already sent to Google cannot be recalled by this site.

Recipients, overseas processing, and retention

When you allow a category, the relevant information is sent over HTTPS to Google LLC and Google’s related services for Google Analytics 4 and, on Shop only, Google Ads. Google may collect data through a regional collection center and process it in other locations, including outside South Korea and the United States; the collection center can vary with the visitor’s location. Google documents that it uses the IP address to derive location at collection and discards it before logging. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s regional data collection information.

In the current GA4 property settings, event-level data is retained for two months; user-level data is retained for 14 months and the period resets with new activity. These settings affect user-level and event-level data, while most standard aggregated reports are not affected. Google Ads conversion records follow Google Ads’ applicable settings and terms; this site does not keep a separate copy of them.

Your rights and contact

You can refuse or withdraw optional measurement as described above. To ask about personal information processed by this site, contact say@seori.studio. Links from this site to Etsy, Smart Store, Instagram, or other third-party services are governed by those services’ own privacy notices.

For Google’s handling of information, use Google’s privacy controls and policy.
